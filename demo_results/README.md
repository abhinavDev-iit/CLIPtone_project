# Official pretrained CLIPtone results

`original.jpg` is the NASA astronaut sample. `warm.jpg`, `cold.jpg` and `high_contrast.jpg` were produced from the same `sample_images/astronaut.png` by the official CLIPtone architecture and released RN50 text-adaptor/AiLUT checkpoints, using the prompts “Warm photo.”, “Cold photo.” and “High contrast photo.”.

`comparison.jpg` shows original | warm | cold | high contrast, left to right. Earlier preset examples have been replaced. These are actual pretrained-model outputs; no training or prompt-specific hand-tuning was performed.
